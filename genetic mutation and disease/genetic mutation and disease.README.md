# Role of Genetic Mutation in Human Disease

This is a basic biology project about how genetic mutations can contribute to human diseases. I explored examples of genetic disorders and learned how changes in DNA can affect the function of proteins.

## Topics Covered
- Sickle cell disease and the HBB gene
- Cystic fibrosis and the CFTR gene
- Huntington's disease and CAG repeats in the HTT gene

## What I Learned
This project helped me understand the relationship between genetic mutations, genes, proteins, and human diseases. It also improved my basic knowledge of molecular biology and genetics.
